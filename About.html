My story

My journey into design started in the physical world. Training in architecture taught me how to think in systems, map complex user flows through physical spaces, and design with deep empathy for human behavior. I loved creating spaces where people lived and interacted, but I found myself increasingly drawn to the speed, interactivity, and scalable impact of digital products.

The transition to Product Design felt natural. Instead of brick and mortar, I now build digital environments using design systems, scalable component architecture, and clean front-end code. My background gives me a distinct edge: I view digital interfaces through a structural lens, ensuring that every product is as functionally sound as it is visually engaging.
